# Social Engineering Attacks: Types, Case Studies, and Prevention Strategies

## Introduction

### What is Social Engineering?

Social engineering is a cybersecurity attack method where attackers manipulate people into revealing confidential information, providing access, or performing actions that compromise security. Instead of directly exploiting technical vulnerabilities, attackers exploit human behavior and emotions such as trust, fear, curiosity, and urgency.

### Why It is Dangerous

Social engineering attacks are dangerous because they target the human element of security. Even organizations with strong technical defenses can be compromised if employees are tricked into clicking malicious links, sharing credentials, or providing sensitive information.

### Statistics

According to the Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report (DBIR), the human element continues to play a major role in many data breaches, with social engineering techniques such as phishing being among the most common attack methods.
